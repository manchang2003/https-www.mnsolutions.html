Diploma in Computer

A Diploma in Computer usually refers to a Diploma in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or Information Technology. It is a short-term course (usually 1-3 years) that provides foundational knowledge in computing, programming, networking, and software applications.

Result Analys

Result Analysis refers to the process of evaluating and interpreting data from exams, tests, or business performance to gain insights and make informed decisions. It is commonly used in education, business, and research to assess performance, identify trends, and improve future outcomes.
