Cyber Security Lessons

Lesson 1: Introduction to Cyber Security

Learn the fundamentals of cyber security, including threats and protective measures.

Text Lesson: Cyber security involves protecting systems, networks, and programs from digital attacks.

Lesson 2: Understanding Malware and Threats

Learn about different types of malware and cyber threats, and how to defend against them.

Text Lesson: Malware includes viruses, worms, and trojans that can harm your computer and steal data. Cyber security practices help prevent these attacks.
