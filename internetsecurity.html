Internet Security Lessons

Lesson 1: Introduction to Internet Security

Learn the fundamentals of internet security, including online threats and protective measures.

Text Lesson: Internet security involves protecting personal data, devices, and networks from online threats.

Lesson 2: Understanding Phishing and Scams

Learn about different types of online scams and phishing attacks, and how to defend against them.

Text Lesson: Phishing is a type of cyber attack where attackers trick users into revealing sensitive information. Learn how to spot and avoid these threats.
